# How an Apple Works

An illustrated systems essay that uses an apple to explain how a system turns cheap, scattered energy into something dense and valuable.

**Live page:** https://seanman005.github.io/THE-APPLE/

## The main idea

An apple tree takes sunlight, water, and air, which are free and spread thin, and refines them step by step into a seed, which is small, dense, and built to last. Each step uses the output of the step below it.

## The five stages

1. **Sunlight, water, air:** free and everywhere, but useless unless caught
2. **Sugar:** energy the tree can store and send where it's needed
3. **Fruit flesh:** sugar packed into one place, as payment for animals to carry the seed
4. **Ripe apple:** the signal that the seed is ready, timed so animals eat it only then
5. **Seed:** the real product. Everything else is packaging

## Key lessons

- **Built from the bottom, directed from the top.** The tree builds cheap layers first and commits to fruit only once they work, but every layer exists to serve the seed.
- **A reliable base makes precision possible.** Thousands of roots and leaves each do one predictable job, which gives the top of the system a steady supply.
- **Input quality sets the limit.** In a poor season, the same tree makes small, sour apples. The machine isn't broken; it ran out of fuel before finishing.
- **Some output pays for outside help.** The tree can't move, so it spends its best output, the fruit, paying animals to carry its seeds.
- **Only one output lasts.** Leaves fall and fruit rots. The seed is the one product that survives and starts the whole engine again somewhere new.

## The question it leaves you with

In whatever you're building, which output is the seed, the one result that lasts and compounds, and which outputs are just packaging?

## Related ideas

- **Energy quality and thermodynamics:** concentrated energy can do more work than diffuse energy
- **Liebig's law of the minimum:** growth is limited by the scarcest input
- **Seed dispersal by animals:** fruit evolved as a reward for carrying seeds
- **Supply chains and value chains:** raw materials refined into products step by step
